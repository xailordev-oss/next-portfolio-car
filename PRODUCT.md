# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, including recruiters and engineering leads, assessing the owner for a full-stack developer role.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents the owner as a full-stack developer. Success means a hiring manager wants to take the candidacy further, for example by getting in touch or moving to an interview.

## Positioning

Full-stack developer: builds whole products, from backend to UI.

**Undecided:** what sets the owner apart from other full-stack developers. Do not invent a differentiator; ask the owner.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Undecided:** the site's sections and how visitors make contact.

## Brand Commitments

**Undecided:** the owner's public name and voice. `xailordev` is the git username, and has not been confirmed as a public name.

## Evidence on Hand

The owner reports having both of the following. Neither is in the repo yet, so the content must come from the owner:

- Shipped projects: live work or repos with links, screenshots or demos.
- Client or job history: named employers or clients and a publishable CV.

The owner did not report having the following, so they must not be fabricated or implied:

- Testimonials or metrics, including quotes, results and numbers.
- Writing, talks or other public work.

## Product Principles

- Serve the hiring manager's assessment first: the site exists to help them judge the owner's fit for a full-stack role.
- Show evidence rather than claims: lean on real shipped projects and work history.
- Show the full stack: present work so that both backend and UI ability are visible.
